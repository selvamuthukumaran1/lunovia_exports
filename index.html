About Us

Lunovia Exports Pvt Ltd is a diversified export company from India dealing in high‑quality agri products, spices, edible oils, textiles, minerals, tea, coffee, marine and eco‑friendly products. We aim to deliver excellence and sustainability through every shipment.

Vision: Delivering India’s natural bounty worldwide with integrity and innovation.

Mission: To be a trusted partner for global buyers by ensuring quality, timely delivery, and ethical trade practices.

Why Choose Us? Rigorous quality control, certified standards, customized packaging, and strong farmer & supplier partnerships.

Export Process

1. Inquiry & Quotation

Share your requirements and receive a tailored quotation covering product specs, packaging, logistics, and timelines.

2. Order Confirmation

On approval, a formal purchase order confirms payment terms, delivery schedules, and specifications for signature.

3. Quality Inspection

QC checks at raw input, in‑process, and final stages ensure compliance with international standards.

4. Packaging & Labeling

Export‑grade packaging with compliant labeling for product details, handling, and certifications.

5. Logistics & Documentation

Customs clearance, certificates of origin, phytosanitary, and shipping documents handled end‑to‑end.

6. Shipment Tracking

Real‑time tracking with regular updates on location and ETA via our portal.

7. After‑Sales Support

Post‑delivery guidance, documentation assistance, and prompt resolution of any concerns.

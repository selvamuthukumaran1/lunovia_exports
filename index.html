How We Work / Export Process

1. Inquiry & Quotation

You send requirements and specifications; we respond with a detailed quotation that includes pricing, lead times and shipping options tailored to your needs.

2. Order Confirmation

After quote approval we issue a formal purchase order covering payment terms, delivery schedules and product specifications for your confirmation.

3. Quality Inspection

Quality checks are performed at multiple stages — sourcing, processing and final packing — to ensure compliance with international standards.

4. Packaging & Labeling

We use export-grade packaging and correct labeling (handling instructions, barcodes, certification marks) to meet destination country requirements.

5. Logistics & Documentation

Our team handles customs clearance, certificates of origin, phytosanitary certificates and all shipping documents to ensure smooth export transit.

6. Shipment Tracking

We provide tracking updates and estimated arrival times so you can monitor your cargo during transit until delivery.

7. After-Sales Support

We continue support post-delivery for handling guidance, documentation help and prompt resolution of any concerns.
